Sending money in and out

  • Money you send from abroad into an NRE or FCNR account is fully repatriable later.
  • From NRO balances you can send up to USD 1 million per financial year after tax, usually with Forms 15CA and 15CB. See repatriation.
  • Compare the rate your bank or remittance service quotes with the mid-market rate above; the difference is their margin.

Know when the rules change

One short email when SEBI, RBI or the tax department changes something that affects NRI investing. Nothing else.
