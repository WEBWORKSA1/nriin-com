Before you courier anything: fund house policies change without notice. Confirm with the fund house or its registrar (CAMS or KFintech) that it still accepts residents of your country, and ask for the current NRI and FATCA forms. If you are a US person, read PFIC and Indian mutual funds first.

Seen a change? Tell us

Readers are the fastest source of policy changes. If a fund house accepted or rejected your application, or switched between online and paper, report it. We verify each report before updating the directory and credit contributors who want to be named.

How we maintain this list

  • Starting list: fund houses named in at least two independent public sources.
  • Every day our system fetches each fund house's website and compares the passages that mention the US, Canada or FATCA. A change raises a re-verify flag on that row.
  • Reader reports are checked against the fund house's own forms before we edit a row.
