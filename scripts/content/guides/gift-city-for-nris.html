GIFT City, in Gandhinagar, is India's International Financial Services Centre — a separate regulatory zone where products are priced in foreign currency and supervised by the IFSCA rather than SEBI and RBI.

What is on offer

ProductTypical minimumWho it suits
Retail USD mutual fund schemes (often feeder funds)From about US$500 on some platformsInvestors who want India exposure without rupee accounts

Alternative investment funds (AIFs)Around US$150,000Accredited and high-net-worth investors

Why NRIs look at it

  • No rupee conversion on the way in or out.
  • No NRE/NRO or PIS setup for most products.
  • Some fund houses that will not onboard US or Canada residents in India do offer GIFT City products to them.

What to watch

  • PFIC for US persons: pooled GIFT City funds are still PFICs. A PMS that holds shares in your own name avoids pooling.
  • Platform risk: you are relying on a smaller set of distributors and platforms; check the IFSCA registration of anyone you deal with.
  • Costs: feeder structures add a layer of expense.
