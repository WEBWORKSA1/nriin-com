Non-residents can buy and sell Indian shares for delivery, apply for IPOs and, with extra setup, trade equity futures and options. A few things remain off limits.

IPOs

  • Apply through ASBA from your NRE account (repatriable) or NRO account (non-repatriable), using the non-resident category in the form.
  • Allotted shares are credited to the matching demat account and count toward the same ownership limits as shares bought on the exchange.
  • Some issues restrict or exclude non-resident applications — read the offer document.

Futures and options

  1. Trading must be funded from NRO (non-repatriable) money and settled in rupees.
  2. You need a custodial participant (CP) code through a clearing member, linked to your broker.
  3. Expect higher margins and position limits specific to non-residents.
  4. Profits remain in NRO and fall under the NRO repatriation rules and Indian tax.

Not allowed

  • Intraday trading in the cash market — every purchase must be taken into delivery.
  • Short selling shares you do not own.
  • Commodity derivatives.

Derivatives are high risk. This guide explains what is permitted; it is not a suggestion to trade them.
