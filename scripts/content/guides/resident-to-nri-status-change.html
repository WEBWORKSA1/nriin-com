Leaving India for work or study changes how every one of your Indian financial accounts must be held. Banks, brokers and fund houses expect you to tell them — not the other way round.

The update checklist

  1. Bank accounts: ask your bank to re-designate resident savings accounts as NRO. Open an NRE account for money you will send from abroad.
  2. Demat: open an NRO (non-repatriable) demat and transfer existing holdings into it. Close the resident demat.
  3. Mutual fund folios: change the tax status from Resident to NRI and replace the bank mandate with your NRO or NRE account — through MF Central or each fund house's registrar.
  4. SIPs: cancel mandates on the old resident account and register new ones on the NRO/NRE account.
  5. KYC: update your address, tax status and contact details at the KRA, and make sure your email is verified.
  6. FATCA/CRS: submit fresh self-certifications showing your new country of tax residence.
  7. Income Tax Department: update your residential status so your PAN stays operative.
  8. Insurance, PPF and NPS: check each product's rules — some can continue, some cannot accept new contributions.

US and Canada movers: some fund houses do not service residents of these countries. After you update your address, they may restrict further purchases. Check the fund house directory before you rely on an SIP continuing.

Timing

Do this within weeks of leaving, not at the next redemption. A folio still marked “resident” can be frozen when you try to redeem, and tax may be withheld at the wrong rate — fixing it from abroad takes far longer than updating it on time.
