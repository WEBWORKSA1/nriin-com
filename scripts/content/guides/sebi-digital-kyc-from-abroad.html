Until now, a digital KYC for the Indian securities market assumed you were physically in India. SEBI's circular of November 2026 drops that assumption for individuals living in countries that meet FATF standards, and it takes effect on 3 December 2026 ().

Who it covers

  • Non-Resident Indians, OCI cardholders and foreign nationals living abroad — SEBI calls them individual “persons resident outside India”.
  • Clients of every SEBI-registered intermediary: brokers, depository participants, mutual fund registrars, portfolio managers and others.
  • Only residents of FATF-compliant countries. If you live in a country on FATF's high-risk list, the earlier process — including the India-location requirement — still applies.

What you will need

  • PAN — verified directly against the Income Tax database, so you do not upload a copy.
  • Passport, and your OCI card if you have one.
  • One officially valid document showing your overseas address. Foreign nationals whose document has no address can use another foreign-government document or an embassy letter.
  • Email and mobile, verified by OTP where possible.
  • If you cannot show an original: a DigiLocker or issuer-verifiable e-document, Aadhaar e-KYC, or an attested copy (notary, Indian embassy or consulate, magistrate or judge, or an authorised official of an overseas branch of an Indian bank).

How the video verification works

The in-person verification moves to a recorded video call with your consent, random prompts, live GPS and timestamp, a face-liveness check and a face match against your documents. Your IP address must resolve to India or the FATF-compliant country you declared. You sign the form electronically and also sign by hand on camera.

Practical tip: intermediaries remain responsible for due diligence and may ask for more than the minimum list. Keep a notarised passport copy and a recent bank statement showing your overseas address ready, even if you hope to finish entirely online.

What to do before 3 December

  1. Check your current status on MF Central or a KRA site. If you are already Validated you may not need to do anything.
  2. If you are On Hold or Registered, decide whether to fix it now under the old process or wait for your broker or fund house to switch on the new digital route.
  3. Make sure the name on your PAN matches your passport exactly — mismatches are the most common reason video KYC fails.
  4. Confirm your passport has at least six months' validity.
