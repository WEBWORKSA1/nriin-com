For UAE residents, India is usually the only country taxing your Indian investments. That makes the Indian side — withholding, treaty documents and refunds — the whole game.

What works

  • Indian mutual funds from nearly every fund house, online.
  • Direct Indian shares through an NRI demat with a designated NRE account.
  • NRE and FCNR deposits — tax-free in India and, with no UAE personal income tax, effectively tax-free.
  • GIFT City USD products, funded in dollars.

Getting treaty relief

  1. Apply for a Tax Residency Certificate from the UAE Federal Tax Authority (it requires proof of residence days and a tenancy contract).
  2. File Form 10F on the Indian e-filing portal.
  3. Send both, with a beneficial-ownership declaration, to fund houses and banks paying you dividends or interest.
  4. File an Indian return each year to recover excess withholding on capital gains.

Watch your India days

Many UAE-based NRIs travel home often. Spending too many days in India — combined with high Indian income — can make you an Indian tax resident or deemed resident. Run the residential status calculator each year.
