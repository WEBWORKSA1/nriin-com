Why NRI folios get blocked

Every fund house and broker in India checks your record at a KYC Registration Agency before it processes a transaction. NRIs hit three problems far more often than resident investors: overseas mobile numbers that never received a verification OTP, PANs flagged as inoperative because the tax department still records the holder as a resident, and passport-based KYC that was captured but never validated against an official database.

Until 30 April 2026, NRIs with “Registered” status were treated as validated. That relaxation has ended, so a status that worked last year may now stop a new purchase. Read the full KYC status guide.

The digital route from abroad

From 3 December 2026, SEBI lets residents of FATF-compliant countries complete KYC and video verification without being in India, using PAN verification, passport, OCI card and an overseas address document. Brokers and registrars will switch it on at their own pace. Details in our digital KYC guide.

Questions

Does this tool connect to my KYC record?

No. It works only from your answers, in your browser. Check your live status on MF Central or a KRA website, then use this tool to decide what to do.

Can someone fix my KYC for me?

You must sign and verify yourself, but a specialist can prepare the forms, check attestation and chase the registrar. Use expert help if you would rather not do it alone.

Will fixing KYC change my tax status?

No. Tax status (Resident or NRI) is a separate field on each folio. Update both. See moving abroad.
