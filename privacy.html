Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026. NRIin.com (“NRIin”, “we”) is operated by the owner of the NRIin.com domain. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Information you type into a form — such as your name, email, country, phone or WhatsApp number, the help you need and any details you add. We ask you not to include PAN, passport, Aadhaar or account numbers.
  • Tool answers — the KYC checker, path finder, PFIC check, document check, tax and status calculators run in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us unless you submit a form (for example, to email yourself a plan).
  • Usage data — pages viewed, referring site, approximate location from IP address, device and browser type, collected through cookies and similar technologies by our analytics and advertising providers.
  • Local storage — small values in your browser such as your cookie choice, the page you first arrived on and cached exchange rates.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and, when you ask for expert help and tick the consent box, to introduce you to one specialist who can help.
  • To send rule-change alerts if you subscribe (unsubscribe at any time).
  • To understand which tools and guides are useful and fix problems.
  • To show advertising that funds the site.

Who we share it with

  • Form processing: form submissions are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which processes them on our behalf.
  • Specialists: only when you request help and consent, we share your request with one vetted professional. They become responsible for how they use your details.
  • Analytics: if enabled and you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity measure site usage.
  • Advertising: see below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. If you choose “Necessary only” in our cookie notice, we request non-personalised ads. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may also see Google's consent message.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and applications: up to 24 months. Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe. Job applications: up to 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, California privacy law or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your information, withdraw consent, or object to processing. Use our contact form and choose “Privacy or data request”. We respond within 30 days.

Children

NRIin is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security and transfers

We use HTTPS and limit access to submissions. Our service providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States and India, with appropriate safeguards.

Changes

We will post updates here and change the effective date. Material changes will be highlighted on the homepage.

Know when the rules change

One short email when SEBI, RBI or the tax department changes something that affects NRI investing. Nothing else.
