Rule changes and dates

  1. 3 Dec 2026 Starts soon KYC

    Digital KYC from abroad goes live

    SEBI's November 2026 circular lets NRIs, OCIs and foreign nationals in FATF-compliant countries complete KYC and video verification without being in India.

  2. 1 Oct 2026 In effect Tax

    No TAN needed to buy property from an NRI

    Resident individual buyers can deduct and report tax on purchases from NRI sellers using their PAN, simplifying property sales by non-residents.

  3. 12 Jun 2026 In effect Stocks

    Listed shares opened to all overseas individuals

    FEMA amendment raises the individual limit to 10% and the aggregate limit to 24%, and extends the route beyond NRIs and OCIs.

  4. 30 Apr 2026 In effect KYC

    “Registered” KYC relaxation ended

    The last extension treating NRIs with Registered status as Validated ran to this date. Check your live status before investing.

  5. 1 Apr 2026 In effect Tax

    Income-tax Act, 2025 takes effect

    The new Act replaces the 1961 Act. Non-resident withholding moves to section 393; “tax year” replaces previous year and assessment year.

  6. 1 Feb 2026 In effect Stocks

    Union Budget 2026 announces higher limits

    The Budget proposed doubling the individual limit for overseas individuals investing in listed Indian shares.
