Capital gains and TDS estimator

For non-residents selling Indian shares or redeeming mutual funds in tax year 2026-27, under the Income-tax Act, 2025. See the withholding you should expect and the tax actually due.

Sets the surcharge (capped at 15% on equity gains).
TaxNot yet estimated

Enter your dates and amounts to see the holding period, rate, withholding and possible refund.

Estimates only. Surcharge and cess are simplified; treaty relief, losses and grandfathered costs are not modelled. Not tax advice.

The rules this estimator uses

  • Listed shares and equity funds become long-term after 12 months. Short-term gains: 20%. Long-term gains: 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh in the tax year.
  • Debt and other specified mutual funds bought on or after 1 April 2023: always short-term, taxed at slab rates; non-resident withholding is typically 30%.
  • Unlisted shares: long-term after 24 months at 12.5%; short-term at slab rates (we assume 30% for withholding).
  • 4% health and education cess applies on tax plus surcharge.

Withholding agents generally do not apply the ₹1.25 lakh exemption or net off your other losses, so many NRIs are owed a refund. Read the TDS guide and treaty relief guide.

Know when the rules change

One short email when SEBI, RBI or the tax department changes something that affects NRI investing. Nothing else.
