Repatriating money from India: NRO limits, Form 15CA and 15CB

How much you can send abroad from NRE and NRO accounts, the USD 1 million rule, and when you need a CA certificate before your bank will remit.

Last reviewed 25 Nov 2026 by the NRIin editorial desk. 6 minute read.

Getting money into India is easy. Getting it out depends on where it sits and where it came from.

The limits

SourceHow much can go abroad
NRE and FCNR(B) balances, and sale proceeds of investments made on a repatriation basisWithout limit, after applicable tax
Current income in NRO (rent, dividends, interest, pension)Freely, after tax
Other NRO balances, including sale proceeds of property and non-repatriable investmentsUp to USD 1 million per financial year, per person

The two forms

  • Form 15CA — your online declaration on the e-filing portal giving the remittance details. Its parts depend on the amount and whether the payment is taxable.
  • Form 15CB — a chartered accountant's certificate confirming the nature of the payment and that tax has been paid, typically needed for taxable remittances above ₹5 lakh in a year.

Forms are being renumbered under the Income-tax Act, 2025; your bank may refer to them by new numbers, but the process is the same.

Step by step

  1. Make sure tax on the money has been paid or withheld (keep the TDS certificate).
  2. Ask a CA for Form 15CB if your bank needs it.
  3. File Form 15CA online, quoting the 15CB acknowledgement where relevant.
  4. Submit the bank's remittance request (often called Form A2) with the 15CA acknowledgement.
  5. Or, for NRO-to-NRE transfers, ask the bank to move the funds internally; the same declarations apply.

Watch the exchange rate. Banks typically add a margin to the market rate. Compare the rate quoted with today's market rate on our INR rates page before you confirm a large transfer.

Questions people ask

Is the USD 1 million limit per person or per account?

Per person, per financial year (April to March), across all NRO accounts and asset sales together.

Can I repatriate sale proceeds of shares bought from NRE money?

Yes. Investments made on a repatriation basis can be sold and the proceeds credited back to your NRE account or remitted abroad, after tax.

Why does my bank want a CA certificate for a small transfer?

Banks apply their own thresholds and risk checks. For amounts that are taxable and above ₹5 lakh, a CA certificate is normally required; for smaller amounts many banks accept only the self-declaration.
