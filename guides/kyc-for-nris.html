NRI KYC status explained: Registered, Validated, On Hold (2026)

What each KRA status means for NRIs and OCIs, why folios get frozen, and the exact steps to reach KYC Validated in 2026.

Last reviewed 25 Nov 2026 by the NRIin editorial desk. 7 minute read.

Every Indian fund house, broker and depository checks one central record before it lets you transact: your KYC status at a KYC Registration Agency (KRA). For non-residents that status is the single most common reason a mutual fund purchase, SIP or redemption gets blocked.

The five statuses and what each one lets you do

StatusWhat it meansNew purchasesRedemptions
ValidatedPAN, address, email and mobile verified against official records.Any fund house, any intermediaryYes
RegisteredDetails captured, but not independently verified (common for NRIs who used a passport instead of Aadhaar).Usually only with fund houses you already hold; new ones may ask for re-KYCYes
On HoldOne or more attributes failed verification — typically email/mobile, an inoperative PAN or a document mismatch.BlockedOften blocked
RejectedThe KRA refused the application, usually over a defective or mismatched document.BlockedBlocked until fixed
Under processThe KRA is still checking your documents.WaitExisting holdings unaffected

The 2026 change that caught many NRIs out. For two years, NRIs with only “Registered” status were treated as if they were “Validated”. That relaxation was extended in stages and was last set to run until 30 April 2026. Do not assume it still applies — check your live status before you place a new purchase.

How to check your status (two minutes)

  1. Go to MF Central or any KRA site (CVL KRA, CAMS KRA, KFintech/Karvy KRA, NDML or NSE KRA) and choose the KYC status enquiry.
  2. Enter your PAN. The result shows the status, the KRA holding your record and, for On Hold, the attribute that failed.
  3. Note whether your email and mobile show as validated. A foreign mobile number that never received an OTP is the most frequent cause of On Hold for NRIs.

How to move to Validated

If you are On Hold because of email or mobile

Use the KRA's modification facility (or the fund house/registrar portal) to re-verify your email by OTP. Email is the more reliable channel for overseas investors because some foreign numbers fail SMS delivery.

If your PAN shows as inoperative

NRIs do not have to link Aadhaar with PAN, but if the Income Tax Department still treats you as a resident your PAN can become inoperative. Update your residential status with the department (via the e-filing portal or your jurisdictional assessing officer, with passport and visa or OCI copies). Once the PAN shows as operative, ask the KRA to revalidate.

If the problem is a document

Resubmit KYC with a current passport, your OCI card if you hold one, an overseas address proof, PAN and a recent photograph. Copies must be attested by a notary, an Indian embassy or consulate, or an authorised official of an overseas branch of an Indian bank. Then complete in-person or video verification.

From 3 December 2026, SEBI's new circular lets NRIs, OCIs and foreign nationals living in FATF-compliant countries complete the whole KYC — including video verification — from abroad. See our guide to digital KYC from abroad.

Do not forget the tax status flag

KYC status and tax status are different fields. If you moved abroad after investing, every folio must be updated from “Resident Individual” to “NRI”, with an NRE or NRO bank mandate. A folio left on resident status can be frozen at redemption, and tax may be withheld at the wrong rate. Our status-change guide walks through it.

Questions people ask

Is my money lost if my folio is On Hold?

No. Your units stay in your name and keep their market value. On Hold blocks new transactions (and often redemptions) until the KYC record is fixed.

Do NRIs have to link Aadhaar with PAN?

NRIs are exempt from the Aadhaar–PAN linking requirement. Problems arise when the Income Tax Department still records you as a resident; then your PAN can show as inoperative and must be corrected by updating your residential status.

How long does revalidation take?

Once the KRA receives complete documents, status usually updates within 2 to 10 working days. Missing attestation is the most common reason it takes longer.
